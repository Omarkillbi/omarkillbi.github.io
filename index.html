Privacy & Compliance

Driver Anonymization System

AI-powered privacy protection for toll violation evidence — GDPR compliant across Europe

Anonymization Demo

How It Works

When a toll violation is captured by roadside cameras, the system receives the raw image containing the vehicle, license plate, and visible occupants. Before the image is attached to any debt collection notice or legal document, the AI anonymization pipeline processes it in real-time.

YOLO-based detection identifies driver and passenger faces & bodies
Smart blur algorithm preserves vehicle evidence while obscuring personal identity
GDPR compliant — meets European privacy standards for automated image processing
Real-time processing — integrated into the debt collection workflow pipeline
98% detection accuracy on driver and passenger anonymization
Custom labeling pipeline for fine-tuned object detection and quality assurance

Business Impact:

This system enables the debt collection company to include photographic evidence of toll violations in customer communications without violating privacy regulations. Previously, images had to be excluded entirely or manually redacted — a slow, error-prone process. The automated solution reduced processing time from hours to seconds while ensuring full legal compliance.
